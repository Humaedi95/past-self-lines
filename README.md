## The entry

A two-line warning sent back to one specific moment: a phone call at 4 p.m. on the last Friday of Ramadan in 2019, when saying yes to covering a night shift meant missing a grandmother's last sahur — and the empty chair at her table is why no Ramadan night shift is ever taken again.

## How it works

`main.ts` (Node 22) writes exactly two lines of English text to `/output/entry.txt` — 256 characters total, within the 1–280 character gate.

Run it:

```sh
node --experimental-strip-types main.ts
```
